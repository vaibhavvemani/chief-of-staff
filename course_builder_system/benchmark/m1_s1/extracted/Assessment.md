Q1. Risk vs. Uncertainty
Which statement best distinguishes risk from uncertainty?

A
B
C
D
Risk involves losses; uncertainty involves gains.
Risk can be measured probabilistically; uncertainty cannot.

Answer: B — Risk can be measured probabilistically; uncertainty cannot. This distinction is foundational to Financial Risk Management.

SECTION A — CONCEPTUAL
Q3. Market Risk vs. Credit Risk
Market risk differs from credit risk because:

A
B
C
D
Market risk is continuous; credit risk is event-driven.
Credit risk cannot be measured.
Market risk does not affect portfolios.
Credit risk only affects banks.

Answer: A — Market risk fluctuates continuously with prices and rates, whereas credit risk is triggered by discrete events such as default or downgrade.

Key Takeaways

Risk Exists in Layers
Market, liquidity, and governance risks interact — no single risk type operates in isolation.

Leverage Is a Double-Edged Sword
At 30:1, Lehman had no buffer to absorb losses when asset values declined.

Illiquidity Kills Faster Than Insolvency
Models and controls are only as effective as the risk culture and alignment behind them.

FRM Is Structural, Not Optional
Financial Risk Management must be embedded into every strategic and operational decision.
