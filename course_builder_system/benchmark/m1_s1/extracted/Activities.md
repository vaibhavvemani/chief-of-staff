**Activity 1 — Personal Risk Mapping**

Reflect on a real company (e.g., airline, tech company, bank).

Identify:

* 2 market risks
* 2 credit risks
* 1 liquidity risk
* 1 operational risk

Write a 1-page analysis explaining why each qualifies.

**Activity 2 — News-Based Risk Analysis**

Choose a recent financial news article.

* Identify the type of risk involved.
* Was it measurable risk or uncertainty?
* Could better risk management have reduced the impact?

**Activity 3 — Risk vs Uncertainty Reflection**

Think of a recent global event (e.g., geopolitical conflict, pandemic).

Classify:

* What aspects were measurable risk?
* What aspects were pure uncertainty?

**Activity 4 — Governance Evaluation**

Research a company’s annual report.

* Does it have a CRO?
* How is risk governance structured?
* What risks does it emphasize most?

**Activity 5 — Case Reflection (Lehman)**

Answer in 300 words:

Which risk category was most critical in Lehman’s collapse?
Was the failure quantitative or managerial?
