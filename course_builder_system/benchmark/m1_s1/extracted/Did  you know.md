DID YOU KNOW?
The CRO Role Barely Existed Before the 1990s
It may surprise you that the formal Chief Risk Officer role did not exist in most banks before the 1990s. Risk management was often decentralised, handled within business units rather than at an institutional level.

1990s Derivatives Boom

Increased complexity demanded more structured oversight.
LTCM Collapse — 1998

Demonstrated dangerous systemic interconnections across institutions.
2008 Financial Crisis

Fundamentally reshaped governance expectations across the industry.

The CRO Today

A Seat at the Top Table
An Evolving Discipline
Today, the CRO sits alongside the CEO and CFO, reflecting how central risk governance has become to institutional leadership.
This evolution shows that Financial Risk Management is not static — it evolves in direct response to crises and growing complexity. Each major shock has reshaped the profession, raising standards and expanding its scope.
