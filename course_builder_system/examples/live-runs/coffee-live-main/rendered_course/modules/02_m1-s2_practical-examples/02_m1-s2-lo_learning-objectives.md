## Content

After studying this section on practical examples in coffee making, you will be able to:

1. Describe how a practical example traces coffee from bean to cup as a connected chain of everyday decisions.
2. Explain how the bean-handling steps of extraction, drying, roasting, and storage shape a cup's taste, texture, and caffeination.
3. Apply this knowledge to make deliberate buying and storage choices, such as selecting a roast level and keeping beans fresh.
4. Match a brewing method—drip, pour-over, espresso, French press, or cold brew—to your available time, equipment, and desired taste.
5. Analyze a given morning scenario and justify which brewing method best fits the stated constraints.
6. Evaluate when to consult a trusted reference such as the National Coffee Association's AboutCoffee.org to guide an uncertain roast or method decision.
