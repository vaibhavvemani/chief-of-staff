## Content

After studying this content on Coffee Making Practice, you will be able to:

1. Explain what coffee making practice means by describing how your knowledge of the coffee bean connects to the brewing decisions you make.
2. Identify the recommended learning path for coffee, beginning with the coffee plant and its agriculture, then moving to grinding, brewing, and roasting.
3. Distinguish among common brewing methods—drip, pour-over, espresso, French press, and cold brew—and describe how each produces a different result.
4. List the practical decisions a learner controls when brewing, including choice of brewing method, the grind, storage, and equipment cleaning.
5. Explain how the storage of coffee beans affects coffee's taste, texture, and caffeination, and describe simple storage habits that protect quality.
6. Apply these decisions to a realistic scenario by selecting a brewing method that fits your time, budget, and taste, and justifying why it suits a beginner.
7. Locate reliable coffee education resources for consumers and students to continue learning about origins, brewing methods, and the science behind coffee.
