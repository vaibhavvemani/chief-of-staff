All About Coffee
Who knows more about coffee than you, our National Coffee Association community? No one—and that’s why we’ve shifted most of our general About Coffee content from this site to our new consumer website, AboutCoffee.org . We’re making it even easier for the public to find reliable, engaging information about coffee—from origins and types of coffee to brewing methods , independent research on coffee and health , and more.
AboutCoffee.org has been developed as a resource for consumers, health professionals, journalists, students, and others. In that capacity, we hope the site also serves as a resource for you as you manage your content needs and promotions.
Grab a cup and happy browsing!
